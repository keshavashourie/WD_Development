Job Clock Off

The Job Clock Off page enables technicians to sign off or indicate that the job has been completed. This transaction is usually used by the assigned technician to indicate that he has stopped working at the current job stage. Clocking off does not necessarily means the end of the entire job. The technician is allowed to clock on and off many times throughout the lifetime of the job.

This transaction can be performed in any job stage within the Job Model but it allowed only if the status is IN PROGRESS.

Note: The Auto Clock Off option is also available on the Job Progress page.

How to Clock Off to a Job

Follow these steps to manually clock off to a job:

1. Open the Job Clock Off page from the Resource > Jobs > Job Services menu. The Job Clock Off page appears.
2. Select the Resource field. The application populates the page fields if it finds a job for the resource.
3. Click Submit on the command bar. The application saves the data and displays a status message.
