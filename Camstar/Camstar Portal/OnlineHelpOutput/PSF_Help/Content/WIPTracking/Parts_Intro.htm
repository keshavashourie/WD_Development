Parts Management

This chapter explains how to create and manage parts. Parts are vital physical components used to run resources or equipments. Ensuring availability and readiness of parts can greatly reduce operation time and cost especially during unscheduled breakdowns.

You must configure the required modeling objects prior to creating parts. The required modeling objects are Resource Material Part and Resource BOMs. Resource Material Part is a required field when a new Part is created that helps to categorize maintenance characteristic of parts based on manufacturing process or business requirement.

Optionally, you can create other modeling objects to describe the parts by defining Physical Location and Physical Position to trace where parts are stored or inventoried. Refer to the Opcenter Execution Medical Device and Diagnostics Modeling User Guideor the Opcenter Execution Core Modeling User Guide for information.
