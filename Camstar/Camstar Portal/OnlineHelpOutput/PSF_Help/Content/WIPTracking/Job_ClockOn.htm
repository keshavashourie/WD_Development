Job Clock On

The Job Clock On page is usually used by the assigned technician to indicate the start of work for the job. A technician is not allowed to start working on the job without clocking on. This process helps to indicate the job has been started and the status of the job will change to IN PROGRESS.

Note: The Auto Clock On field is also available on the Job Acknowledge page.

How to Clock On to a Job

Follow these steps to manually clock on to a job:

1. Open the Job Clock On page from the Resource > Jobs > Job Services menu. The Job Clock On page appears.
2. Select the Resource field. The application populates the page fields if it finds a job for the resource. Ensure that the technicians have acknowledged the job using the Job Acknowledge page.
3. Click Submit on the command bar. The application saves the data and displays a status message to indicate the Job Order has been successfully clocked on.
