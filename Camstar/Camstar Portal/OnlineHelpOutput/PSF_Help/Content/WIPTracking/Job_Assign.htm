Job Assign

The Job Assign page enables you to assign maintenance or repair jobs to technicians. The page can also be used to change or remove an existing assignment.

How to Assign a Job

Follow these steps to assign a job to technician:

1. Open the Job Assign page from the Resource > Jobs > Job Services menu. The Job Assign page appears.
2. Select the Resource field. The application populates the page fields if it finds a job for the resource.
3. Click Add New Row icon above the Technicians grid. The application enables a new Technician field on the grid.
4. Select a technician from the Technician field list.
5. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
6. Click Submit on the command bar. The application saves the data and displays a status message.
