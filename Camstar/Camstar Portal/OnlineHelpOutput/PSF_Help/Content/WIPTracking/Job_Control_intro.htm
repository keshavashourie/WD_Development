Job Control

Job Control transactions enable you to manage resource maintenance or repair work. Resources are any machines, equipments, tools, carriers, materials, or any other entities that you can configure to mimic and drive your operation model. A job refers to any task that focuses on the resource such as routine maintenance, repair, or upgrades that must be fulfilled for smooth manufacturing operation.

Job Control transactions enable you to create, assign, and track resource maintenance or repair jobs in shop floor manufacturing to ensure the required maintenance steps are executed in the predefined sequence.

You must configure the required job control modeling objects before using the shop floor job control transactions. The required modeling objects are Job Models (the workflow to perform the job), Job Order and Job Steps. Optionally, you can create other modeling objects to describe the repair or maintenance by defining the Job Symptom Code, Job Cause Code or Job Repair Code. Refer to the Opcenter Execution Medical Device and Diagnostics Modeling User Guideor the Opcenter Execution Core Modeling User Guide for information.
