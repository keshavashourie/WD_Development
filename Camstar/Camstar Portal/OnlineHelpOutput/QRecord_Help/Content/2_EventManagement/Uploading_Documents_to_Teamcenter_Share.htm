Uploading Documents to Teamcenter Share

You can upload documents to Teamcenter Share from the Manage G/E and the Manage P/E pages if you are a licensed Teamcenter Share user and are logged into the application using the SAM single sign-on mode of authentication. The application displays the Upload to Share button when these conditions are met.

Use the Upload to Share button to upload documents (Word documents, images, etc.) to share and collaborate with other users and team members. These documents can be downloaded from the Teamcenter Share console after they are successfully uploaded.

History of the documents uploaded to Teamcenter Share is available in the History Mainline grid, accessible through the Mfg Audit Trail page.

Refer to the Siemens technical support website, Siemens Support Center, for information on Teamcenter Share.

Note: The Upload to Share button is disabled if the production event or the generic event is completed and the status is Closed.
If the event is reopened, the button is available once again and you can upload documents to Teamcenter Share.

How to Upload Documents to Teamcenter Share

Follow these steps to upload a document to Teamcenter Share:

1. Access the quality record. The record's management page appears.
2. Click Upload to Share in the Quality Actions pane. The Attach to Share page appears.
3. Select a Project.
4. Select a Document Path.
5. Enter a Document Name.
6. Enter Document Description as necessary.
7. Click Submit. The application displays a message indicating the document was uploaded to Teamcenter Share and displays the event’s management page.

How to View Documents Uploaded to Teamcenter Share

Follow these steps to view documents uploaded to Teamcenter Share:

1. Access the quality record.The record's management page appears.
2. Select the attachment you want to download.
3. Click View Attachment in the Summary/Details pane. The attachment begins downloading.
