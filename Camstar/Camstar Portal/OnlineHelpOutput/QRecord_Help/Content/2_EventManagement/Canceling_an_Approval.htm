Canceling an Approval

Use the Cancel Approval action to cancel the approval process for a quality record resolution. You can cancel the process at any time regardless of whether approvers have entered decisions.

You can cancel an approval when:

• The status of the record is In Review.
• You are the owner of the record.

The application disables the Cancel Approval button when either of the above conditions are not met.

Note: You can view canceled approval cycles on the Resolution tab of the record’s management page and on the Sign Approval page when the approval request is routed subsequently.

Refer to "Canceling Approvals" for information on canceling approvals.
