Defining a Job Model

Job Model is a flow or series of steps that needed to be performed for the resource's repair or maintenance. This modeling object allow you to predefine the necessary details such as the job types, stages to perform the repair, execution details, attaching the necessary document and more.

A Job Model can be a simple one step workflow or a complicated multi-steps workflow. There will be validation to ensure that the required maintenance steps are performed and in the correct sequence.

The application supports three Job Types:

• Normal. As the name implies this is a normal maintenance Job order.
• PM is for job in which during the Job Complete transaction, the auto completed maintenance will be auto-selected.
• Recondition. The part’s recondition count will be auto-incremented during the Job Complete transaction.

When a Job Model is linked to any open or active jobs, you are not allowed to perform the following actions:

• Delete an existing stage
• Edit an existing stage sequence
• Add a new stage
• Delete the Job Model modeling object

You must complete or cancel the open jobs to perform above actions.

After configuration, a job model can be assigned to a specific equipment resource as the procedure to follow if the equipment fails.

How to Define a Job Model

Follow these steps to define a Job Model:

1. Open the Job Model page. The Job Model page appears within the Modeling page.
2. Click New. Blank fields appear for you to define a new instance.
3. Enter a name for the Job Model field.
4. Select Job Type.
5. Use the Details grid as follows for each stage (step) to be added to the job model:
a. Click Add new row. The application opens the Add pop-up.
b. Select Stage.
c. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
6. Click OK. The Add pop-up closes.
7. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to "Common Fields on Modeling Pages" for information on the fields common to all modeling objects.
8. Click Save. The application saves the modeling object and displays a success message.
