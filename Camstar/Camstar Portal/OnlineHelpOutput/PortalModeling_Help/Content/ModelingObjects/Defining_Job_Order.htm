Defining a Job Order

A Job Order communicates required maintenance that is due for a resource. This page helps to identify resource’s maintenance due and enables you to assign technician to execute the maintenance or repair job.

How to Define a Job Order

Follow these steps to define a Job Order:

1. Open the Job Order page. The Job Order page appears within the Modeling page.
2. Click New. Blank fields appear for you to define a new instance.
3. Enter a name for the Job Order field.
4. Select the Job Model.
5. Enter optional information according to your business requirements.Refer to "Common Fields on Modeling Pages" for information on the fields common to all modeling objects.
6. Click Save. The application saves the modeling object and displays a success message.
