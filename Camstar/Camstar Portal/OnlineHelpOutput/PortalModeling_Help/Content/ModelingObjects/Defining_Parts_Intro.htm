Part Definitions

Part is a physical object that is often used on resources or equipment. Parts can be stored in inventory, used on equipment, and returned back to inventory. Multiple parts can share a resource material part and have the same maintenance characteristics.

Part modeling objects help to define, update, and track manufacturing resource material parts before a part can be created. To ensure that all parts have a ground set of common characteristics, at least one resource material part must be assigned to each part. This enforced association helps to ensure valid part substitutions and enables individual parts to be tracked by type or resource material part.

A Part is created using the Part Create shop floor page and can be further maintained in the part's shop floor pages such as Part Setup, Part Maintenance, and more. Refer to the Opcenter Execution Medical Device and Diagnostics Shop Floor Guide or the Opcenter Execution Core Shop Floor Guide for information.

To access the part shop floor pages from the menu bar, navigate to Resource > Part or Resource > Part Services.

Note: Resource Material Part in this chapter refers to material part for resource-not Material Parts that is used for product.
