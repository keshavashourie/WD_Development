Lot Bonus

Lot Bonus is used to add bonus quantity to either the Qty or Qty 2 of a lot. The transaction will either add to Qty only or both Qty and Qty 2 depending on whether the lot is in a quantity or item processing step.

How to Add Bonus Quantity to a Lot

Follow these steps to add bonus quantity to a lot:

1. Access the Lot Bonus page.
2. Select the lot using the Selection Id field. The application displays the lot details in the Lot Info grid. It also displays wafers in the Items grid if the lot is in an item-processing step.
3. Is the selected lot in an item processing step?

If . . .

Then . . .

Yes, the lot is in an item-processing step
a. Click Add new row on the Details grid. A row is activated in the grid.
b. Select the Item Id.
c. Select the Bonus Reason.
d. Enter the Qty being added in the Bonus Qty field.

No, the lot is quantity based.
a. Click Add new row on the Details grid. A row is activated in the grid.
b. Select the Bonus Reason.
c. Enter the Qty being added in the Bonus Qty field.
4. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
5. Click Submit. The application displays a success message.
