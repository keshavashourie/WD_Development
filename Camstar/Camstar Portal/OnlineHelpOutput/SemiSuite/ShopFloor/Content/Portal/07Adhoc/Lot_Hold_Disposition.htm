Lot Hold Disposition

Lot Hold Disposition enables you to release a hold on a lot, assign a processing plan to it, and transact it. There are three Lot Hold Disposition transactions:

• Lot Hold Disposition - Dispose & Transact: Enables you to release a lot hold, assign a processing plan (dispose), and immediately transact the lot.
• Lot Hold Disposition 1 (Dispose Only): Enables you to dispose and manually transact later.
• Lot Hold Disposition 2 (Transact Only): Enables you to transact a previously disposed lot. The disposition must have been performed using Lot Hold Disposition 1.

How to Perform a Lot Hold Disposition

Follow these steps to use any of the three Lot Hold Disposition pages:

1. Access the appropriate Lot Hold Disposition page.
2. Enter the lot Id in the Selection Id field. The application displays the lot in the grid.
Note: The Selection pop-up may contain a search parameter labeled NEW DISPOSE (Y/N). A Y value returns only lots with no Disposition. An N value returns lots with and without a Disposition.
3. Enter a reason for why you are releasing the hold in the Release Reason field.
4. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
5. Click Submit. The application displays a success message.
