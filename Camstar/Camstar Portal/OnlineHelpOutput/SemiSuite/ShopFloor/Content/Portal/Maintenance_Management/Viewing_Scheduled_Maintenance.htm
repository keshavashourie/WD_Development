Viewing Scheduled Maintenance

The PM Schedule Overview page enables you to view scheduled maintenance for selected resources. The page displays up to ten resources schedules per page. Paging is enabled when there are more than ten.

PM Schedule Overview Page

The PM Schedule Overview page display varies considerably based upon filters and criteria you select.

Date Selection

Schedule Start Date and Schedule End Date are required. However, the date range can be ignored for Date and Recurring Date requirements by selecting the Show All PMs check box.

Date Selection Edits

• Schedule Start Date cannot be after Schedule End Date.
• Schedule End Date cannot be before Schedule Start Date.
• Schedule Start Date and Schedule End Date cannot be the same.
• Date range cannot be more than one year (to keep page rendering performance acceptable).
Note: Throughput and Usage PMs will be displayed regardless of the date range selection.

Time Scale

Date based PMs can be displayed using the following Time Scale options:

• Day – full 24 hour day block (Columns are with the first initial of the day – M, T, W, T, F, S, S.)
• Week – 7 day block with Monday as the 1st day of the week (Columns are labeled with W followed by week number, for example, W1, W2.)
• Month (Columns are labeled with abbreviated month name, for example, Jan, Feb, Mar.)

Recurring scheduled date PM requirements are displayed as a line of segments where each segment represents either a day, a week or a month; depending on the scale selected.

Throughput PMs are always be rendered on a scale of ten segments. Each segment represents 10% of the overall throughput requirement.

Color Codes

Date and Recurring DateRequirement:

• Green – No PM occurrences within segment
• RED – One or more PM occurrences within segment

Throughput Requirements:

• YELLOW – Remaining available throughput
• RED – Processed throughput

Usage Requirements:

• YELLOW – Remaining counts available
• GREEN – Count already used

Hover Behavior

When you are viewing Date or Recurring Date requirements, you can hover the cursor over a segment (block) to see actual dates.
