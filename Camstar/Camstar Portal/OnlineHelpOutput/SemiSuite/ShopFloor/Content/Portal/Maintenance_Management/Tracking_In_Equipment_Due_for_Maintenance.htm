Tracking In Equipment Due for Maintenance

By default, equipment that is due for maintenance cannot be Tracked In. Two maintenance requirement configuration options—Waive and Delay—provide the WIP Main operator with some flexibility.

Waiving Outstanding Maintenance Requirements

The WIP Main operator can waive scheduled maintenance due for a resource if the maintenance requirement is configured with the Waive option. Waiving a requirement means that the requirement due for a particular resource does not have to be performed. This does not affect future instances of the same requirement becoming due again for the same resource.

Delaying Outstanding Maintenance Requirements

The WIP Main operator can delay scheduled maintenance due for a resource if the maintenance requirement is configured with the Delay option and a configured maximum has not been exceeded. Refer to "Modify Maintenance" for information.
