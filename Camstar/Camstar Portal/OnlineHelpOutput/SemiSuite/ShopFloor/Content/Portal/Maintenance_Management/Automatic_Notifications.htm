Automatic Maintenance Notifications

By default, Opcenter Execution Semiconductor prevents equipment use, initiates configured maintenance notifications, and creates configured maintenance jobs when a WIP Main operator attempts to Track In equipment for which maintenance is past due or due within a defined warning period. Efficiency can be added to this process for date and recurring date requirements by configuring business rules to automatically send e-mail notifications and create maintenance jobs when maintenance becomes due rather than waiting for the operator to interact with the user interface.

Note: Refer to the Opcenter Execution Semiconductor Modeling Guide for information.
