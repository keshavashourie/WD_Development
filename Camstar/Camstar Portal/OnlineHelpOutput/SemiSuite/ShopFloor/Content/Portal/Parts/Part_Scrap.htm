Part Scrap

The Part Scrap page enables you to to scrap quantity from a part. The application will automatically decommission the part once the entire quantity is scrapped.

How to Scrap Part Quantity

Follow these steps to scrap part quantity:

1. Access the Part Scrap page.
2. Select a part in the Part field. The application fills in the page fields.
3. Enter the quantity that you want to scrap in the Scrap Qty field.
4. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
5. Click Submit. The application displays a success message.The message indicates that the part has been decommissioned if all quantity of the part has been scrapped.
