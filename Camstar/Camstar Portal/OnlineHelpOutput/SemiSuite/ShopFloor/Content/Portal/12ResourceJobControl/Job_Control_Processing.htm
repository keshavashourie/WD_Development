Job Processing

Jobs are created and assigned to one or more technicians. Technicians perform each stage (step) configured for the job. The job is ready for the supervisor to execute the Job Complete transaction when a technician has completed the last configured step.

Note: Some steps involve creating a part request. A supervisor cannot perform a Job Complete transaction for a job with an outstanding part request.
Job Status

Job Status indicates the job's current point in the Job Control process flow. These are the possible statuses listed in progressive order:

• CREATED – job has been created but not yet assigned
• ASSIGNED – default status of newly assigned jobs available to the technician
• ACKNOWLEDGED – technician has acknowledged but not started the job
• CANCELED – supervisor canceled the job
• INPROGRESS – technician is currently Clocked On (working on) the job
• ACTIVE – technician has Clocked On for the job at least once but is not currently Clocked On
• COMPLETED – supervisor has executed the Job Complete transaction
