Job Clock On

There are two mutually exclusive ways a technician can clock on to a job.

• Automatically by selecting Auto Clock On in the Job Acknowledge window
• Manually by using the Job Clock On transaction from the Technician Jobs page

How to Manually Clock On to a Job

Follow these steps to manually clock on to a job:

1. Access the Technician Jobs page.
2. Select the job from the list. The Job Clock On button will be enabled if you did not select Auto Clock On when you acknowledged the job.
3. Click Shop Floor Transactions. The application displays a list of transactions.
4. Click Job Clock On. The Job Clock On pop-up appears.
5. Click Job Clock On. A status message appears and the Job Clock On pop-up closes. The job status shows on the list as INPROGRESS.
