Job Assign

The supervisor can assign a job from the Job Create window or from the Job Assign window by clicking Job Create or Job Assign respectively on the Supervisor Jobs page.

How to Assign a Job

Follow these steps to assign or reassign one or more technicians to a job from the Supervisor Jobs page:

1. Access the Supervisor Jobs page.
2. Filter the list for a Job Status of CREATED to see unassigned jobs.
3. Select the job.
4. Click Shop Floor Txns. The application displays a list of transactions.
5. Click Job Assign. The Job Assign pop-up appears.
6. Click the technician name (or blank area where the name would be) on the Technicians grid. Select the technician from the enabled field.
7. Click Job Assign. A status message appears; the pop-up closes and the Job Status shows as ASSIGNED.
