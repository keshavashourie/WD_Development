Viewing Job History

Resource job history for a selected job can be viewed from the Supervisor Jobs page and from the Technician Jobs page by clicking the Job History icon on the selected row of the Search Results grid.

How to View Job History

Follow these steps to view job history:

1. Select the job row on the Search Results grid.
2. Click the Job History icon on the selected row. The application displays the Job History pop-up with history for the selected job.
3. Click a history item row in the upper grid of the pop-up if you want to view related history detail. The application populates the lower grid of the pop-up with the history detail.
