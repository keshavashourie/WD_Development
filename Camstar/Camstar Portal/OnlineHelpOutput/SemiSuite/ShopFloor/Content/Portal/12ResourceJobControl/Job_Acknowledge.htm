Job Acknowledge

The technician uses the Job Acknowledge transaction to acknowledge an assigned job. This transaction is available from the Technician Jobs page by clicking the Job Acknowledge button which brings up the Job Acknowledge pop-up.

How to Acknowledge a Job

Follow these steps to acknowledge a job:

1. Access the Technician Jobs page.
2. Select the job from the list. The Job Acknowledge button becomes enabled if the job is not yet acknowledged.
3. Click Shop Floor Txns. The application displays a list of transactions.
4. Click Job Acknowledge. The Job Acknowledge pop-up appears.
5. You can select Auto Clock On to have the application automatically clocks you on and bypass manually performing the Job Clock On transaction.
6. Click Job Acknowledge on the pop-up. A status message appears and the Job Acknowledge pop-up closes. If you selected Auto Clock On, the job status will be INPROGRESS. If you did not, the status will be ACKNOWLEDGED.
