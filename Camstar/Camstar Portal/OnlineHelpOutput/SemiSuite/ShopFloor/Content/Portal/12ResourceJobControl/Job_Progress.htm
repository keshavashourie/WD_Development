Job Progress (Completing Stages)

The Job Progress transaction becomes available for a job after the technician has acknowledged the job and clocked on. This transaction brings up the Job Progress pop-up for the stage that currently needs to be completed.

How to Complete a Job Stage

Follow these steps to complete a job stage or step:

1. Access the Technician Jobs page.
2. Select the job from the list.
3. Click Shop Floor Txns. The application displays a list of transactions.
4. Click Job Progress. The Job Progress pop-up appears.
Note: A step may have required fields, checklists and data collections that are not initially in view. Use vertical scrolling and collapse the Technicians and Maintenance Statuses grids to better see what is on the window.
5. Select the appropriate Symptom Code, Cause Code, and Repair Code if these fields are enabled.
6. For each checklist listed in the Checklist grid:
a. Select the check box for the grid. The row is highlighted in blue.
b. Scroll below the Checklist grid to see any data collections required. The data collection points appear below the grid.
c. Enter each required data point.
d. Click Job Progress. The highlighting color of the selected checklist row changes to green.
7. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
8. Click Part Request to bring up the Part Request pop-up if you need to request a part or parts. Refer to "Requesting Parts" for information.
9. Click Job Progress when finished. A results message appears and the Job Progress pop-up closes.
