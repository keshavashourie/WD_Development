Job Control

Opcenter Execution Semiconductor's Job Control module enables you to schedule, administer, and track resource maintenance jobs. Each job is resource specific and contains one or more steps for the assigned technician or technicians to perform. The steps within a job are determined by the underlying Job Model.

Note: The required Job Control modeling objects must be configured before using Job Control. Refer to Opcenter Execution Semiconductor Modeling Guide for information.
