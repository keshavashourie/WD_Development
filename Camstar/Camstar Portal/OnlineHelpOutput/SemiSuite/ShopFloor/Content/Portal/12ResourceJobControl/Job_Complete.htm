Job Complete

The supervisor can access the Job Complete transaction pop-up by clicking the Job Complete button on the Supervisor Jobs page.

Before Performing Job Complete

The Job Complete transaction requires that the job's current step be configured with Allow Complete selected and that there be no unfinished part requests associated with the job.

Job Complete Pop-up Field Definitions

This table defines the fields on the Job Complete pop-up.

How to Complete a Job

Follow these steps to complete a job from the Supervisor Jobs page:

1. Access the Supervisor Jobs page.
2. Select the job from the list.
3. Click Shop Floor Txns. The application displays a list of transactions.
4. Click Job Complete. The Job Complete pop-up appears.
5. Click Job Complete on the pop-up. A status message appears and the pop-up closes.
