Equipment Materials Setup

The Equipment Materials Setup page enables you to specify the equipment plus materials required to Track In a material lot. These materials can be consumable material parts such as substrates and epoxies as well as product material parts such as manufactured dies or prepared substrates. Materials can be serialized or non-serialized.

How to Set Up Materials for a Resource

Follow these steps to set up materials for a resource:

1. Select the equipment from the Equipment field.
2. Enter the lot or batch Id in the Material Lot field.
3. Click the Material Part field to activate it.
4. Enter the material part from the Material Part field.
5. Click Add if the information was not automatically added to the Material Details grid.
6. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
7. Click Submit. The application displays a success message.
