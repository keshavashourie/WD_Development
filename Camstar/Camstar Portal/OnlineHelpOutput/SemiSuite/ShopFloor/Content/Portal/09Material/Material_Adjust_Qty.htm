Material Adjust Quantity

The Material Adjust Qty page enables you to modify the Qty, Qty 2, or both of a Material Lot.

How to Adjust the Quantity of a Material Lot

Follow these steps to adjust the quantity of a material lot:

1. Access the Material Adjust Qty page.
2. Select the material lot using the Selection Id field. The application populates the Lots grid with the selected lot's information.
3. Select the reason for the quantity adjustment from the Adjust Reason field.
4. Enter New Qty, New Qty 2, or both according to your business requirements.
5. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
6. Click Submit. The application displays a success message.
