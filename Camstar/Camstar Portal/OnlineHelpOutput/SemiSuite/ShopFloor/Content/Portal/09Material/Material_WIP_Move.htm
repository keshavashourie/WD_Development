Material WIP Move

The Material WIP Move page enables you to move one or more material lots from one WIP step to another.

Note: Keep in mind that material lots are moved into WIP by the Material Withdrawal transaction.

How to Perform a Material WIP Move

Follow these steps to perform a Material WIP Move:

1. Access the Material WIP Move page.
2. Enter the lot Id into the Selection Id field. The application populates the Material Lots grid with information for the selected lot.
3. Select a To Workflow and revision.
4. Select a To Step.
5. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
6. Click Submit. The application displays a success message.
