Lot Schedule Cancel

Lot Schedule Cancel is used to cancel a schedule. Cancellation requires that there be only one active lot remaining in the schedule. You must combine lots back to the main lot to cancel a schedule containing more than one active lot.

When Canceling a Schedule

The lot or lots must be in one of the following statuses: SCHEDULED, PREPARED, or ACTIVE. The lot must not be on hold, loaded into equipment, or processing in equipment.

How to Cancel a Schedule

Follow these steps to cancel a schedule:

1. Access the Lot Schedule Cancel page for the type of schedule being canceled.
2. Enter the Lot ID in the Selection Id field. The application populates fields with information pertaining to the lot entered.
3. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
4. Click Submit. The application displays a success message.
