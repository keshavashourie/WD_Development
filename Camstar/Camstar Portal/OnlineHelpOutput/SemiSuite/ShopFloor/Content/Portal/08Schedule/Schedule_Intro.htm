Scheduling Transaction Pages Overview

Scheduling is the process of preparing a lot for production. Each lot must have a schedule before it is allowed to proceed from INVENTORY to WIP. The application creates an object to store information for the lot as it proceeds through WIP. This information controls how the lot moves through WIP and will be transferred to history when the lot completes WIP processing.
