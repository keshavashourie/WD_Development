Surveillance

Surveillance requirements enforce data collection for resources, resource groups, operations, and work centers. The WIP Main Advanced Surveillance enables you to view active surveillance requirements in real time. Refresh is not necessary.

The refresh rate is dependent on the schedule of a supporting Scheduled Business Rule within Opcenter Execution Semiconductor Modeling. Contact your System Administrator regarding the refresh rate configuration.

The left portion of the pop-up contains filtering fields. (Filters are mutually exclusive.) The filters will minimize the displayed content based on the selected criteria. The right portion of the pop-up contains a grid that will populate with all active surveillance requirements when the Surveillance pop-up page appears. The Surveillance subscription is performed automatically during the loading of the page. In the event of a Subscription failure it will be necessary to reopen the pop-up. Refer to "Surveillance Requirements" for more information.

How to View Surveillance Requirements

Follow these steps to view surveillance requirements from WIP Main Advanced:

1. Select the Surveillance button on the WIP Main Advanced page. The Surveillance pop-up appears. The application automatically subscribes to the SignalR connection and populates the Status field with Subscribed.
2. Select one or more filter parameters to narrow your search.
3. Click Filter. The application populates the grid with any search results once the first predefined refresh period has elapsed.
Note: You can change your filter criteria without having to click Filter again. The revised search results will appear when automatic refresh occurs.
