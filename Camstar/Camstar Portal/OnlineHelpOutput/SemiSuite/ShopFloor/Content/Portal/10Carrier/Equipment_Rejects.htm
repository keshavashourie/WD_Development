Carrier Equipment Rejects

The Carrier Equipment Rejects page enables you to collect rejects collected from the Equipment when one or more Carriers are processing in the Equipment. This service is similar to Lot Rejects In-Process (WIP Tracking Txn) but it is stored by Equipment only. This will allow the application to auto transfer in-process rejects collected to the next Carrier tracking out from the Equipment.

Rejects collected are stored in the Equipment Rejects grid.
