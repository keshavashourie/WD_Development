Transfer Equipment Rejects

Transfer Equipment Rejects is used to transfer rejects collected in the Equipment to a Carrier lot manually. Rejects must first be collected through the Carrier Equipment Rejects service. Rejects are usually transferred automatically to a lot during track out since they are stored by Equipment only. Rejects can be manually transferred to a lot currently processing in the equipment in special situations.

Rejects transferred will be stored in the lot’s current insertion information.
