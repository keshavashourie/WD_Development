Required Modeling Objects for Inline SPC

The listed Modeling objects below are the required modeling objects to support the definition of Inline SPC charts and recording of user annotations and violations:

• Defining SPC Connections
• Defining SPC Chart Type
• Viewing SPC Violation
• Defining SPC Rules
• Defining SPC Queries
• Defining SPC Charts
• Defining SPC Chart Groups
Note: SPC Violation is loaded during installation and cannot be edited. You may use the SPC Violation page to view the parameters for each violation.
