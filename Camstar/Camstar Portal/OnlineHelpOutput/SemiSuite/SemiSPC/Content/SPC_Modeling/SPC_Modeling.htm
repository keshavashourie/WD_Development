Modeling

For Inline SPC, modeling objects allow you to create custom run-time views for your environment, support the definition of Inline SPC charts and recording of user annotations. The SPC modeling pages can also be used to configure the object instances and to make modifications.

This section contains information about the SPC objects and how instances are configured.
