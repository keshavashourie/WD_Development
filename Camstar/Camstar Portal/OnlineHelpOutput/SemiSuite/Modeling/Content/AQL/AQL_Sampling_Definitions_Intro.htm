AQL Sampling Definitions

AQL sampling inspection is a statistical practice in which a subset of a lot is tested to estimate the characteristics of the whole lot to prevent production of off-quality product. The Acceptance Quality Limits (AQL) Sampling module enables you to model and execute sampling plans for lots. Sampling plans define the number of samples and frequency of sampling required and the associated allowable rejects. AQL Sampling determines whether a lot is accepted or rejected based on the collected sampling data. A lot fails sampling if the number of rejects exceeds the acceptable reject count.

In addition to standard lot-based sampling, Opcenter Execution Semiconductor AQL Sampling enables you to configure wafer-based sampling where, at a specific workflow step, the application samples a specified number of wafers in a lot. The number of wafers is determined by the sample rate as defined by the assignment of the Wafer Selection AQL Level and Wafer Selection Inspection within the Sampling Plan.

Note: Wafer WIP Data Sampling will take precedent over AQL Sampling. If the existing default AQL Sampling and the new Wafer WIP Data Sampling are configured at the same step, only Wafer WIP Data Sampling will be performed. Refer to "Defining Wafer WIP Data Sampling" for information.
