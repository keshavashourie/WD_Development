AQL Modeling Sequence

As you create an AQL sampling model, it is important that you follow a specific sequence of steps. The modeling sequence for AQL begins with defining your inspection levels and ends with using the Sampling Plan Setup matrix object to associate the Sampling Plan to products, specs, or other criteria.

This table shows the high-level steps needed to collect Opcenter Execution Semiconductor AQL Sampling data:

For this step . . .

Define . . .

1

Inspection Level modeling objects to establish sample size codes. Refer to "Defining Inspection Levels" for information.

2

An AQL Level modeling object to specify the acceptable quality levels by associating a sample size and rejection quantity to a sample size code. Refer to "Defining AQL Levels" for information.

3

The sampling information for the WIP Data Setup modeling object . The Sampling Information section of the WIP Data Setup page enables you to create sample tests, which you can group to create sample plans. Refer to "Defining Sampling Information for WIP Data Setup Objects" for information on the Sampling Information section of the WIP Data Setup modeling object. Refer to "Defining WIP Data Setup Objects" for general information on the WIP Data Setup modeling object.

4

A Switching Rule modeling object, if required, to specify the rules that move a sampling plan from one inspection level to another. Refer to "Defining Switching Rules" for information.

5

Sampling Plan modeling objects to organize the sampling activity for a lot. You can define a Sampling Plan for either the entire sample plan, or one for each sample test. Refer to "Defining Sampling Plans" for information.

6

One of the following:

• PN modeling objects to assign a sampling plan to a product. Refer to "Assigning Sampling Plans to Products" for information on the AQL Sampling settings in the PN modeling object.
• Sampling Plan Setup matrix objects to define the criteria the application should use to apply sampling plans to lots. Refer to "Defining a Sampling Plan Setup Matrix" for information.
