Send Ahead Modeling

A Send Ahead lot is a small test lot split off from a mother lot or lots, released to the workflow, and validated. This enables the manufacturer to detect and address issues before releasing the remainder of the lot or lots. The envisioned typical use case is one pilot wafer split from one mother lot. However, you can create a quantity (die) based Send Ahead lot.
