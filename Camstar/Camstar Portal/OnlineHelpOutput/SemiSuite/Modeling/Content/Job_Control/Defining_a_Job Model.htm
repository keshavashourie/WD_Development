Defining a Job Model

A Job Model is a workflow performed for the repair or routine maintenance of a manufacturing resource. The Job Model object is used to configure a Job Model workflow. After configuration, a job model can be assigned to a specific equipment resource as the procedure to follow if the equipment fails.

How to Define a Job Model

Follow these steps to define a Job Model:

1. Open the Job Model page. The Job Model page appears within the Modeling page.
2. Click New. Blank fields appear for you to define a new instance.
3. Enter a Job Model name.
4. Select Job Type.
5. Use the Details grid as follows for each stage (step) to be added to the job model:
a. Click Add new row. The application opens the Add pop-up.
b. Select Stage.
c. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
d. Click OK. The pop-up closes.
6. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
7. Click Save. The application saves the modeling object and displays a success message.
