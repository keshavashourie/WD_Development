Defining a Job Stage

A job stage is a step in a job workflow. The Job Stage modeling object is used to add a job stage.

Note: Although the stage name must be unique, there can be many jobs using the same stage name, each with its own definition of what comprises that stage.

How to Define a Job Stage

Follow these steps to define a Job Stage:

1. Open the Job Stage page. The Job Stage page appears within the Modeling page.
2. Click New. Blank fields appear for you to define a new instance.
3. Enter name of the job stage in the Job Stage field.
4. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
5. Click Save. The application saves the modeling object and displays a success message.
