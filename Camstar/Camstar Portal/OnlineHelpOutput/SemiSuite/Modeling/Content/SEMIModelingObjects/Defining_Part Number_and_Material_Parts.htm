Defining Part Number and Material Part Products

Opcenter Execution Semiconductor distinguishes two categories of "product" using the following nomenclature:

• Part Number (PN) or Product– is a manufacturing output produced for a customer.
• Material Part – is a raw material or component consumed during the manufacturing process.

Technically, a material part is a sub-class of PN. The modeling objects are virtually identical. In fact, a PN product can be used as a material part as long as it has a Material Type assigned.

When Defining PNs or Material Parts

You define a PN on the PN page. You define a material part on the Material Part page. The only difference between the two pages is the title and the label for the first field. The field definitions and modeling instructions below apply when modeling either of these objects.

How to Define a PN Product or a Material Part Product

Follow these steps to define a PN product or a Material Part product:

1. Perform one of the following:

If you want to . . .

Then . . .

Define a new PN product

Open the PN page. The PN page opens within the Modeling page.

Define a new Material Part product

Open the Material Part page. The Material Part page opens within the Modeling page.
2. Click New. Blank fields appear for you to define a new instance.
3. Enter the name for the instance you are defining. The field is labeled either PN or Material Part depending upon which type of object your are defining.
4. Enter the revision of the spec in the Revision field.
5. Click Expand All. The application expands the collapsed sections of the page.
6. Select Product Type.
7. Select Product Line.
8. If you are defining a multi-product (a product comprised of other products):
a. Check Is Multi-Products in the Product Data section.
b. Scroll down to the Item Sub Products grid.
c. Click Add new row on the grid. A new row appears.
d. Select the product (and product revision number) of the sub-product.
e. Repeat steps c–d for each additional sub-product.
9. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
10. Click Save. The application saves the modeling object and displays a success message.
