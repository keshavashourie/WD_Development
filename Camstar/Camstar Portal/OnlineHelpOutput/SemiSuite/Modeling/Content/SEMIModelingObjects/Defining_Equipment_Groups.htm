Defining Equipment Groups

An Equipment Group is used to group individual equipment resources together. There are five types of equipment groups, each corresponding to a manufacturing operation. The five types of equipment groups are:

• Assembly Equipment
• Back Grind Equipment
• Test Equipment
• Wafer Equipment
• Wafer Sort Equipment

When Defining Equipment Groups

You must define equipment that will be part of the equipment group before you can define the group.

In-Process Status Change for Equipment Groups

In-Process status change refers to the ability to change equipment status regardless of whether the equipment is actively processing a lot. In-Process Status Change can be selected at the equipment group level by selecting the In-Process Status Change check box on the Equipment Group page. The following conditions must be met before a group level in-process status change setting will be applied to individual equipment:

• All equipment groups to which equipment belongs must have the same In-Process Status Change setting.
• Any sub-groups within those groups must also have the same setting as the parent groups.
Note: Refer to "Defining Equipment" for information on setting in-Process status change at the equipment level.

How to Define an Equipment Group

Follow these steps to define an Equipment Group:

1. Open the Equipment Group page for the type of equipment that you want to add. The Equipment Group page appears within the Modeling page.
2. Click New. Blank fields appear for you to define a new instance.
3. Enter a name for the equipment group in the [type] Equipment Group field.
4. Perform the following steps to add equipment to the Entries grid:
a. Click Add new row.
b. Select an equipment resource from the list that appears in the grid.
c. Repeat steps a-b for every equipment resource that you want to add to the group.
5. Perform the following steps to add equipment subgroups to the Groups grid:
a. Click Add new row.
b. Select an equipment group from the list that appears in the grid.
c. Repeat steps a-b for every equipment group that you want to add as a subgroup.
6. Click Resolved Entries. The application displays a pop-up listing all of the equipment included in the group.
7. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
8. Click Save. The application saves the modeling object and displays a success message.
