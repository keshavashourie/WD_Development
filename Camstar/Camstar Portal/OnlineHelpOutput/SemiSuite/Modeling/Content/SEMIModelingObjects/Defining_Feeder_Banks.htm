Defining SM Feeder Banks

A feeder bank can hold multiple feeders of consumable material. A feeder bank has a physical slot for each feeder it can accommodate. Feeder banks provide the ability to load one or more feeders before the machine that will consume the material becomes available.

How to Define a SM Feeder Bank

Follow these steps to define a SM Feeder Bank:

1. Open the SM Feeder Bank page. The SM Feeder Bank page appears within the Modeling page.
2. Click New. Blank fields appear for you to define a new instance.
3. Enter a name for the Feeder Bank in the Feeder Bank field.
4. Select the feeder family from the Resource Family field if the feeder bank belongs to a feeder family.
5. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
6. Click Save. The application saves the modeling object and displays a success message.
