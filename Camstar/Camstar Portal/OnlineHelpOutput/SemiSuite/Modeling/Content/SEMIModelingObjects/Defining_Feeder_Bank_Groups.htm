Defining SM Feeder Bank Groups

A Feeder Bank Group is used to group individual feeder banks together.

When Defining SM Feeder Bank Groups

You must define feeder banks that will be part of the feeder bank group before you can define the group.

How to Define a SM Feeder Bank Group

Follow these steps to define a SM Feeder Bank Group:

1. Open the SM Feeder Bank Group page. The SM Feeder Bank Group page appears within the Modeling page.
2. Click New. Blank fields appear for you to define a new instance.
3. Enter a name for the feeder bank group in the Feeder Bank Group field.
4. Perform the following steps to add feeder banks to the Entries grid:
a. Click Add new row. A selection field becomes enabled on the Entries grid.
b. Select a feeder bank from the field list on the Entries grid.
c. Repeat steps a-b for every feeder bank that you want to add to the group.
5. Perform the following steps to add feeder bank subgroups to the Groups grid:
a. Click Add new row. A selection field becomes enabled on the Groups grid.
b. Select a feeder bank group from the field list in the Groups grid.
c. Repeat steps a-b for every feeder bank group that you want to add as a subgroup.
6. Click Resolved Entries. The application displays a pop-up listing all of the feeder banks included in the group.
7. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
8. Click Save. The application saves the modeling object and displays a success message.
