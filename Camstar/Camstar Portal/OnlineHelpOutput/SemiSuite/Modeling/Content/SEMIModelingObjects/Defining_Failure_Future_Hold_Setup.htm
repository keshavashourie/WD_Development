Defining Failure Future Hold Setups

Failure Future Hold Setups are used to define setup details required when placing future holds on lots in the event of a failure.

How to Define a Failure Future Hold Setup

Follow these steps to define a Failure Future Hold Setup:

1. Open the Failure Future Hold Setup page. The Failure Future Hold Setup page appears within the Modeling page.
2. Click New. Blank fields appear for you to define a new instance.
3. Enter the name of the Failure Future Hold Setup in the Failure Future Hold Setup field.
4. Perform the following for every future hold to add in the Future Hold Setup Details grid:
a. Click Add new row. The application adds a blank row to the grid.
b. Enter the spec that details the processing of the future hold in the Spec field.
c. Enter the reason for the future hold in the Hold Reason field.
d. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
e. Repeat steps a-d for every future hold to add.
5. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
6. Click Save. The application saves the modeling object and displays a success message.
