Defining Exp Auto Hold Objects

The Exp Auto Hold modeling object defines a hold reason for a lot hold on an Experiment Plan. When you specify an experiment auto hold for an Experiment Plan and execute the Experiment Plan, the application performs the lot hold and sends an e-mail notification to the specified e-mail group. You can specify multiple holds for a single Experiment Plan.

How to Define an Exp Auto Hold

Follow these steps to define an Exp Auto Hold:

1. Open the Exp Auto Hold page. The Exp Auto Hold page appears within the Modeling page.
2. Click New. Blank fields appear for you to define a new instance.
3. Enter a name for the Exp Auto Hold object in the Exp Auto Hold field.
4. Enter optional information according to your business requirements. Refer to the field definitions table for information on the optional fields.
5. Click Save. The application saves the modeling object and displays a success message.
