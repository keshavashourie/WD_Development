Modeling Objects

Modeling is the part of Opcenter Execution Semiconductor (Opcenter EX SM) used to define and maintain the elements of a manufacturing facility model. These elements contain data such as products, operations, specifications, and workflows that are used to describe and control processing activities.

Modeling objects are the building blocks from which you construct an electronic representation of your factory. They describe and control the manufacturing process in Opcenter EX SM Portal and are further used to control product-producing activities in the factory.

Modeling also includes physical shop floor information such as definitions for enterprises, factories, organizations, roles, resources, and locations. The defined data is then used by Opcenter EX SM Portal to control and manage product processing on the shop floor.

The quality model portion of the Information Model is used for creating modeling objects related to quality issues, for example the objects used to set up Event Recording.

Opcenter EX SM Portal includes standard pages you can use for modeling your manufacturing facility.

You set up your modeling and shop floor users through the Employee modeling object definition. After it is defined, the employee appears in the user list within the employee definition page.
