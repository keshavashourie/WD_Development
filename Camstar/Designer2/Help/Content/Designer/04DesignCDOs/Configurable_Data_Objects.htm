Configurable Data Objects

Configurable data objects (CDOs) are Opcenter EX MDD or Opcenter EX CR objects that can be configured to meet your factory model needs. CDOs represent Opcenter EX MDD or Opcenter EX CR data.

Note: Siemens recommends prefixing customer-specific CDOs to avoid conversion issues during future Opcenter EX MDD or Opcenter EX CR version upgrades. This recommendation extends to Siemens service consultants, Siemens third-party integrators, and customer personnel.
