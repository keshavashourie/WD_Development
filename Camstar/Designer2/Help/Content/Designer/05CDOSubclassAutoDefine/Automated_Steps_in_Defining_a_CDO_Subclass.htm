Automated Steps in Defining a CDO Subclass

Defining a CDO involves more than simply selecting a CDO in the class library and clicking Add. The process of adding a CDO requires fitting the new CDO into the Opcenter EX MDD or Opcenter EX structure. This involves knowing when to create subentities and fields, when to map, when to override, and so on. Most of this process is automated in Opcenter EX MDD or Opcenter EX CR.

This chapter explains the concepts behind the automated procedures. It contains flowcharts and descriptions of the procedures to define Opcenter EX MDD or Opcenter EX CR objects, as well as exercises describing the process.
