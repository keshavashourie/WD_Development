Complete Expressions Specification

Reduced BNF is used for expression specification. Each production rule alternative is shown on a separate line. Non-terminal symbols are enclosed in angle brackets. Terminal symbols are shown as is but surrounded by spaces. Curly braces denote repetition (zero or more times) of the enclosed element.
