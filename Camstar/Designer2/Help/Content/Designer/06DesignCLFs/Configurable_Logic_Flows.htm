Configurable Logic Flows

A Configurable Logic Flow (CLF) is a sequence of functions performing one or more tasks. Logic flows are the way that user-configurable business processes are implemented. Most occur at the server level where the user defines the business rules and logic. (High Performance CLFs are processed on the database rather than the application server.) CLFs are used to uniquely define how Opcenter EX MDD or Opcenter EX CR responds to a CDO event. This chapter covers the concepts related to CLFs within Opcenter EX MDD and Opcenter EX CR.
