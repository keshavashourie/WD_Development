Concurrent Licensing

Customers purchase a finite number of licenses from Siemens when they purchase Opcenter EX MDD or Opcenter EX CR. The number of users concurrently logged into the Portal cannot exceed the number of licenses purchased.

When someone logs into the Portal a session is started and one license is considered in use for that specific user account.

Once the number of users logged in equals the number of licenses available (purchased), no new users will be allowed to log in until a license becomes available (when another user closes a session, for example).

Anyone who attempts to log in once the license limit has been reached will receive an error stating there are no available licenses.

Once users are logged in, they can access all licensed features and modules. Therefore, concurrent licensing is different than feature licensing, which determines what modules have been licensed.

Note: If your company needs more concurrent licenses than what you currently own, please contact Product Support to purchase additional licenses.
