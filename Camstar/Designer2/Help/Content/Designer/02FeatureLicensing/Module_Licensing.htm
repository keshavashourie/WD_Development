Module Licensing

Siemens offers separately licensed modules to customers to meet industry-specific needs. These modules are available, at an additional cost, to customers who purchase Opcenter Execution Medical Device and Diagnostics (Opcenter EX MDD) or Opcenter Execution Core (Opcenter EX CR).

Opcenter EX MDD and Opcenter EX CR customers have the option to purchase a license for separately licensed modules. Refer to the Release Matrix for Opcenter EX MDD on the Siemens Support Center or the Release Matrix for Opcenter EX CR on the Siemens Support Center for a complete list of included and optional features.

Licensing provides you with authorized access to these modules.

This chapter provides information regarding how licensing is incorporated after you have purchased a license for one or more modules.
