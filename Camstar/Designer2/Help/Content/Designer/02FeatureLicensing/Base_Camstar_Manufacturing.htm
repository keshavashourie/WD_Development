Opcenter EX MDD and Opcenter EX CR

Both Opcenter EX MDD and Opcenter EX CR include the industry’s most robust Manufacturing Execution System, as well as the Process Design & Modeling capability that allows you to define, enforce, and audit your manufacturing process.

For example, base Opcenter EX MDD and Opcenter EX CR include:

• Portal for Modeling and Manufacturing Execution
• Designer to configure the application to fit your factory model
• Portal Studio for customizing and maintaining the Portal
• A Manufacturing Audit Trail that provides the complete history of all manufactured lots, batches, and serialized units, spanning production in multiple plants
• Data Collection

Separately Licensed Modules

In addition to base Manufacturing, Siemens offers several separately licensed modules that deliver additional capabilities to customers in several different industries. One or more of these modules can be purchased by customers who have already purchased base Opcenter EX MDD or Opcenter EX CR.

All separately licensed modules are disabled in Opcenter EX MDD and Opcenter EX CR. Refer to the Release Matrix for Opcenter EX MDD on the Siemens Support Center or the Release Matrix for Opcenter EX CR on the Siemens Support Center for a complete list of included and optional features.

Enabling Modules

There are two ways to install separately licensed modules:

• At the initial installation.

If you purchased one or more separately licensed modules, then use the correct license file and serial number for that particular combination of Opcenter EX MDD or Opcenter EX CR and modules during installation.

• After either Opcenter EX MDD or Opcenter EX CR is already installed.

You must obtain a new license and serial number for the module from Siemens.

Refer to the Opcenter Execution Medical Device and Diagnostics Installation Guide or the Opcenter Execution Core Installation Guide for complete installation information.
